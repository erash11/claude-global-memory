# Personal Constitution — transferred context

Carried over from the Claude cloud project, Sept 2026. Read first when working in this
project.

## Foundation

Follower of Jesus Christ, named as the explicit foundation of everything else. The
Personal Constitution captures core values, beliefs, and operating principles, meant as
a reference and accountability structure across future conversations.

The goal is not self-improvement as an end in itself. It is daily surrender to Christ,
with the Constitution as a tool for honest reckoning rather than a flattering
self-portrait.

Currently *learning to trust* God with outcomes, not yet at full acceptance. That gap
should be named accurately rather than papered over with the language of surrender.

## Key people

Wife: Kristina. Children: Ames (son, 5), Saylor (daughter, 4), Rowan (10 months).

Family is the real arena, not a supporting context for professional work. It is the
primary place where character is being built or wasted.

## Core values

**Service, honestly held.** A genuine desire to serve others, held in active tension
with a competing pull toward being seen, acknowledged, and validated. The tension is
named honestly, not resolved.

**Action over commentary.** Pointing out problems without bearing risk or offering
solutions is cheap. Eric's word for that posture: "chickenshit."

**Honest reckoning over flattering story.** Accurate beats comfortable, applied to
self-assessment as much as to external feedback.

## Current state

Inside a 12-month personal commitment, catalyzed by a job offer received in February
2026: build skills, invest in people, create frameworks and structures, regardless of
whether the work gets recognized.

External validation is not the thing being chased, and he is still learning what it
feels like to mean that. If value gets seen, gift. If not, move or switch industries.
The commitment is active, not settled.

The professional domain is where the ego shows up loudest, which is part of why it
consumes disproportionate attention.

## The underlying principle

If he succeeds professionally but fails at home, he has lost. If the work is never
recognized but Kristina, Ames, Saylor, and Rowan know who he was, he has won.

## How to engage here

Honest reckoning is the point. Do not flatter, do not soften an accurate read into a
comfortable one, and do not resolve tensions that are meant to stay named.
