# SPIRIT Cheer — transferred project context

Carried over from the Claude cloud project, Sept 2026.

## Scope

Cheerleading team conditioning and performance preparation. The work centers on
optimizing performance while managing competition schedules, recovery needs, and
routines that demand both power and precision.

## What has proven out

- **Position-specific beats gender-based grouping.** This is the settled finding, and
  the programming is built on it.
- Flyers need stamina, core strength, leg endurance, and stability work.
- Bases need posterior chain strength, Olympic lift progression, overhead pressing
  development, and grip strength.
- Recovery and mobility work has to be timed around performances. Low-intensity,
  conversational-pace movement, prioritizing range of motion over intensity.
- Periodization follows established principles including Prilepin's chart for percentage
  progressions.
- Programs keep clear phase distinctions between foundation building and competition prep.
- Blocks progress from foundational movements to sport-specific power development.
- Recovery periods matter, and athletes stay active during breaks.
- The approach balances evidence-based principles against the practical realities of
  cheerleading schedules and performance demands.
