## Bottom line

Prairie View is not a body-bag opponent. They are a functional SWAC offense with a
dual-threat quarterback who is the single most dangerous player Baylor will see before
Louisiana Tech. Their defense cannot rush the passer against anyone competent.

That combination makes this a near-ideal diagnostic. Baylor's Week 1 offensive problems
(run game, third down, finishing) all get a clean test against a defense that will not
manufacture pressure. And Baylor's one defensive hole from Week 1, edge setting to the
defensive left, gets attacked immediately by a quarterback running 7.0 per carry.

**Do not read this game's havoc rate, sack total, or takeaway count as signal.**

## What to actually watch

The score is not the deliverable. These four are.

**1. Highlight yards per rush opportunity.** Baylor produced 1.50 against Auburn with zero
explosive rushes. Prairie View gave up 5.41 to Tarleton and 6.1 yards per carry. If Baylor's
number does not climb well past 3.0 here, the run game problem is structural rather than an
Auburn-defense problem. This is the highest-value number in the game.

**2. Points per scoring opportunity.** 2.43 in Week 1, five trips inside the Auburn 22 for
16 points. Any drive reaching the Prairie View 40 counts. Against this defense the target is
5.0 or better. Anything under 4.0 confirms the finishing problem is real and not
opponent-driven.

**3. Third down conversion rate.** 32% against Auburn. Prairie View allowed Tarleton to go
7 of 10. Still in the 30s against this defense means play-calling or personnel, not matchup.

**4. Edge setting to the defensive left against QB runs.** Carter's designed keepers and
scrambles to that side. Chart them separately from team rush defense. This is the only
defensive number from this game worth carrying to Week 3.

### Discount or ignore

- Havoc rate, sacks, pressure rate. Prairie View's line surrendered one hurry to Tarleton
  in 93 plays. Baylor's front will look elite regardless.
- Takeaways. Prairie View has zero giveaways in two games, and Baylor's three interceptions
  against Auburn were roughly +14.4 points of turnover luck against 1.3 expected. Turnover
  margin is not a Baylor strength to carry forward.
- Explosive plays allowed and opponent yards per carry.

Compare Week 3 Louisiana Tech back to the Auburn baseline, not to this game.
