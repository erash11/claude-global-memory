## What this data still cannot answer

The Quick form has 5 questions. These were never asked, so the framework's planned
flags cannot be produced:

- Does it involve student-athlete information, and what kind?
- How is it paid for, and when does the contract end?
- **Did it go through the ITS IDEA form?** — the bypass-rate number
- Does the vendor still hold data from discontinued products?

The `athlete_data_likely` column is **my category inference, not a respondent
answer.** Treat it as a screening list, not a finding.

## Judgement calls worth a human eye

See the `review_note` column in `clean/tech_master.csv`:

- **Hudl** — merged "HUDL Statsbomb", "HUDL WyScout" and "HUDL Focus". Same vendor,
  three products. This is why Soccer shows as both using and having stopped Hudl.
- **Whistle** — one unit wrote "Whistle (DataViz)", others wrote bare "DataViz" and
  "GPS DataViz". Assumed to be the same thing.
- **Teamworks** — "TW Nutrition" and "Teamworks Hub" folded in as modules.
- **6-4-3 Charts** — merged Baseball's "643 Charts" and Softball's "6-4-3".
- **Social Media Platforms** — Instagram, TikTok, X and "social media apps
  primarily" bucketed together.
- **Evertz** — the broadcast answer was one run-on list. Only "Routing (Magnum)"
  and "replay (Dreamcatcher)" are read as Evertz; the rest are mapped to their own
  vendors.

- **Red Card** — identified by Eric as the student-athlete meal-money stipend,
  redeemable at selected local vendors. It carries athlete identity and
  transaction records, so it is categorised as athlete data and belongs in the
  detailed second pass even though it is not a performance system.

**Two products could not be identified at all** and are sitting in
`clean/unmapped_review.csv`: **SCRL app** and **JABA**, both named by Brand
Engagement. Eric is checking what they are.

Every other name mapping is explicit in `scripts/aliases.py`. Nothing was guessed
silently.
