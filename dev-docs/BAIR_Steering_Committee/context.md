## Evaluation framework

Five scored dimensions:

| Dimension | Weight |
|---|---|
| Strategic Alignment | 31% |
| Athlete & Staff Burden | 26% |
| Revenue & Resources | 20% |
| Research Quality | 15% |
| Gender Equity | 10% |

Feasibility is a binary gatekeeper, not a scored dimension. Review is dual-track with a
conservative default to full committee review.

## What has proven true in review

- **Athlete burden is the top practical constraint.** High athlete time triggers full
  committee review automatically and carries a heavy scoring penalty. Collapsing
  multi-phase designs or piggybacking on existing sessions dramatically improves viability.
- **Internal PI positioning matters.** Proposals that read as extractive external research
  score poorly. Repositioning with an internal PI plus internal resources changes the
  strategic calculus substantially.
- **"Start strict, loosen later."** Gallucci's principle: you can't undo a yes. This is
  why the review defaults are conservative.
- **Gender equity is an incentive, not an exclusion.** The framework rewards inclusive
  designs rather than penalizing single-gender studies.
- **Plain language beats institutional prose.** Formal, promotional, or bureaucratic
  writing actively undermines credibility with this audience.

## Ways of working

- Oversight runs by email, not meetings. Routine check-ins (Blake's progress) are monthly email.
- MS Teams is BAIR's virtual home. MS Planner for visual project tracking, Excel for
  detailed metrics, built for leadership-friendly visual scanning.
- Formatting is calibrated to audience: dense reference docs for the committee,
  scannable dashboards for leadership.
- Expect multiple revision passes with specific targeted feedback.
- Fireflies retrieves meeting transcripts before drafting follow-ups. Search pattern:
  keyword plus date filter, then fetch by transcript ID.
- Parse uploaded .docx via dedicated extraction, not raw read tools.
- html2pptx / pptxgen for PowerPoint: slide dimensions 960x540px, watch font sizing.
