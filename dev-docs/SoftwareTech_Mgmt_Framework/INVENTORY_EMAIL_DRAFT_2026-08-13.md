## Editing notes — not part of the email

Run through the humanizer skill. Pattern removal only; the skill's "add soul, use
first person, let some mess in" guidance is blog advice and would work against a staff
email that CCs Kenny and Jovon.

**Removed:**
- Two rule-of-three constructions — the "find out late / pay twice / can't answer"
  triple, and the three-bullet block
- Two slogans, both negative parallelisms: "A rough list beats a perfect one that
  never gets sent" and "I'm building a map, not grading anyone"
- Setup filler: "A few notes to make it easier:"
- Hyphens used as em dashes, twice
- "Quick ask" from the subject line
- The bulleted list, folded into prose — bullets in a short email read as a form

**Kept deliberately:**
- **The three-minute figure in the subject.** Biggest single lever on response rate.
- **"Please don't look anything up."** The failure mode is people deferring the email
  until they can compile something complete. Lowering the bar prevents that.
- **"This isn't a review of how anything was purchased or set up."** The other failure
  mode is under-reporting by anyone who suspects something was set up off-process.
  Worth saying plainly even though it survives as a mild negative construction.
- **The offer to talk in person.** Kenny suggested it, and it's likely how football
  and basketball responses actually arrive.
