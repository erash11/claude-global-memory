# P4 Benchmarking — transferred project context

Carried over from the Claude cloud project, Sept 2026.

## Why this exists

A strategic research initiative to inform Baylor Athletics FY27 planning and potential
restructuring. It benchmarks how Power 4 institutions structure their High Performance
and Applied Sports Science departments.

Particular attention goes to reporting hierarchies and their impact on technology
integration and operational effectiveness. The comparative analysis is meant to produce
data-driven insight for optimizing Baylor's departmental organization and performance
outcomes.

## Current state

- Targets all Power 4 institutions
- Blake Wright is lead researcher
- Collection covers reporting structures, administrative layers, technology stacks, and
  staff ratios across competing programs
- A structured outreach strategy exists, using Blake's position as a PhD student
  researcher to encourage more candid responses from peer institutions
- Framework combines quantitative collection (Excel dataset) and qualitative analysis
  (white paper)
- Completion targeted mid-March to align with strategic planning cycles

## Method notes

Outreach is framed through an academic researcher perspective rather than direct
institutional comparison. That framing is deliberate: it encourages transparency that a
Baylor-administrator-to-peer-administrator ask would not get.

Methodology emphasizes systematic collection across standardized metrics while
maintaining professional networking protocols within the athletics performance community.

Communication balances detailed project scope against concise actionable requests,
depending on audience and relationship context.

## Tools and sourcing

Structured Excel workbooks for contact management and data organization. Multi-tab
systems track outreach across conference divisions: Big 12, SEC, Big Ten,
ACC/Independents.

Contacts come from athletics staff directories, LinkedIn professional networks, and
conference connections. Key targets are Directors of Sports Science, High Performance,
and Strength & Conditioning at peer institutions. Contact information and institutional
athletics website resources are documented systematically.
