# Room size floor of ten, falling back to a combined big-men room

A room needs at least ten linemen with usable data. Because Room Deviation is
expressed in population standard deviations (ADR 0004), only the room *mean* is
required — never the room's own standard deviation — and the standard error of
that mean is 1/sqrt(n) in the same units. Ten athletes therefore gives roughly
±0.32 SD, and that is conservative: a position group's internal spread is likely
tighter than the general population's.

If the offensive line falls short, offensive and defensive linemen are combined
into a single "big men" room and the finding is renamed to say so plainly.

## Considered and rejected: falling back to the normative population

Comparing athletes against the S2 normative mean instead of a room mean would
collapse Room Deviation back into the percentile the project exists to get
beyond. The fallback must preserve a room, or there is no finding.

## Consequences

Committing to this now is the point. A thin room discovered mid-analysis is
exactly when a floor gets quietly rationalized away.
