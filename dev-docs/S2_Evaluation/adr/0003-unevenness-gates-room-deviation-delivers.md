# Profile Unevenness gates; Room Deviation is the finding

The kill criterion in ADR 0001 and the Position Fit decision frame were testing
two different quantities. Profile Unevenness is within-athlete and references no
one else; Room Deviation is between-athlete. An athlete can be extremely uneven
in exactly the way every offensive lineman is uneven — high Unevenness, zero
Room Deviation — so passing the kill criterion would not, on its own, have said
anything about position fit.

These are therefore nested rather than parallel. **Unevenness is the gate**: if
profiles are flat there is nothing for anyone to deviate on, and the project
stops. **Room Deviation is the finding**: it answers the Position Fit question
that motivated the work.

## Consequences

The finding is distributional, which makes room size a first-order constraint —
a central tendency cannot be defined from a handful of athletes. This is why the
offensive line was chosen over safeties or linebackers, reversing an earlier
recommendation made on cognitive-demand grounds before the nesting was settled.
