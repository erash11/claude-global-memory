- Plain language for leadership audiences. Documents must sound human and
  conversational. "AD ready" means simple, direct, natural.
- The "Articulation Skill" is the named approach for polishing documents into Eric's
  authentic voice. Offer it when delivering drafts.
- Work runs iteratively across sessions, building on prior documents and integrating new
  research or components into existing frameworks.
- Deliver comprehensive, ready-to-use artifacts: complete Word docs, full PowerPoint
  files, not outlines.
- Feedback of "sounds AI-generated" means do a plain-language rewrite.
- Favor practical application over theory. Content should be immediately actionable.
- Apply structured frameworks consistently: phased timelines, Kirkpatrick, curriculum maps.
